### Overview
This project developed a predictive analytical framework to forecast daily energy generation from wind and solar sources, translating technical outputs into a strategic **ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance)** investment roadmap. The objective was to provide institutional investors with a data-driven tool to mitigate infrastructure risk and optimize green portfolio performance.

### The Technical Challenge
Renewable energy yield is inherently stochastic, fluctuating based on complex environmental variables. To deliver a reliable forecast, the project addressed:
* **Data Heterogeneity:** Cleaning and normalizing multi-dimensional weather datasets, including variables like wind speed, humidity, and cloud cover.
* **Predictive Accuracy:** Building models that account for seasonal variance and environmental noise to ensure short-term grid stability.

### The Solution: Python-Driven Analytics
I architected an end-to-end pipeline in **Python** to manage the full data lifecycle:
* **Feature Engineering:** Leveraged **Pandas** and **NumPy** to process raw weather data and identify high-impact predictors for energy yield.
* **Predictive Modeling:** Developed and compared **Multiple Linear Regression** and **Random Forest** algorithms to predict generation across a 24-hour window.
* **Statistical Rigor:** Performed residual analysis and addressed multi-collinearity to validate model robustness against diverse weather scenarios.

### ESG Strategic Integration
Beyond the numbers, the technical insights were integrated into a strategic framework for institutional decision-making:
* **Risk Mitigation:** Analyzed grid stability risks to help investors identify and avoid potential "stranded assets" in volatile regions.
* **SDG Alignment:** Mapped generation efficiency directly to **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, providing transparent metrics for impact reporting.
* **Investment Roadmap:** Authored an advisory report for C-level stakeholders on leveraging AI to inform long-term infrastructure and outreach strategies.

### Results & Impact
* **Operational Efficiency:** Delivered a "one-click" forecasting tool that simplifies complex environmental data into actionable energy yield predictions.
* **Strategic Clarity:** Provided a dual-layered outcome: high-accuracy technical models for engineers and a clear ESG roadmap for financial analysts.
